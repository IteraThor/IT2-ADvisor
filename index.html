🗺️ IT2 System Architecture & Relationship Map

How the IT2 system components physically mount, interconnect, and route power and data:

🎯 1. Mechanical Foundation

  • Winmau Plasma Standard: IT2 camera arms match the Plasma hole pattern 1:1 with zero adapters.
  • Target Corona: Requires 3x 3D-printed conversion brackets to adapt magnetic legs.
  • DIY Light Rings: Standard 360° or Low-Ceiling (≤ 2.0m flat arch) both share the same arm mounts.

🧱 2. The Baseplate Hub

  • Wall Impact: Direct Wall requires ~8 holes; Baseplate requires only 3 holes.
  • Stand Support: Winmau Xtreme 2 mobile stand natively mounts straight to the baseplate.
  • Integrated Features: Internal cable routing channels, TPU 95A sound dampeners, rear hardware bay.

📷 3. Vision Architecture

  • Classic 3-Camera Rig: 3x HBV 32x32 USB boards (OV9732 budget / OV2710 premium) mounted at 120°.
  • Autodarts Lens: Uses a single smartphone in a calibrated ring mount; eliminates arms, USB cables, and external PC.

🔩 4. Fasteners & Assembly

  • Heat-Set Inserts: M4/M6 brass inserts melted into plastic; reusable for lifetime disassembly.
  • Direct Self-Tapping: M4 screws tap straight into plastic pilot holes; €0 inserts and no soldering iron needed.
